# Scenario SC-01 — Successful room invitation

**Feature:** FE-02 · **Use case:** UC-06

## Summary
The room owner successfully invites an existing user who is not already a member of the room. This process validates the ownership, room existence, invitee existence, and membership status before adding the invitation.

## Given
- The user (`cl`) is the owner of the room (`room_data`).
- The invitee (`new_member`) exists.
- The invitee is not currently a member of the room.

## When
- The room owner calls the `SendRoomInvite` function, providing the room details and the invitee details.

## Then
- The room adds the invitee to its `invites` map.
- The invitee adds the room to their `room_invites` map.
- The invitee receives a message: "Received an invite to join room: <room_name>".
- The owner receives a message: "Invite sent to <invitee_nick>".

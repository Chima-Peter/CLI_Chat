# Scenario SC-01 — View pending room invites with invited users

**Feature:** FE-02 · **Use case:** UC-07

## Summary
The room owner views the list of users who have been invited to the room and have not yet accepted or declined the invitation. This is applicable when there are active invitations.

## Given
- The current room exists.
- The room owner has sent invitations to one or more users.
- The invitations are currently pending in `room.invites` (`server/rooms.go:282`).

## When
- The room owner calls the `SeePendingRoomInvites` method (`server/rooms.go:291`).

## Then
- The system collects the nicknames of all invited users from the `r.invites` map (`server/rooms.go:295-297`).
- The system sends a message to the room owner listing the invited users (`server/rooms.go:299`).

## Step-by-step

| Step | Actor / system | Expected | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Room owner | Invokes `SeePendingRoomInvites` | `server/rooms.go:291` |
| 2 | System | Acquires RLock on room | `server/rooms.go:292` |
| 3 | System | Iterates over `r.invites` to collect nicknames | `server/rooms.go:295-297` |
| 4 | System | Sends message with numbered list of invitees | `server/rooms.go:299` |
| 5 | System | Releases RLock | `server/rooms.go:293` |
